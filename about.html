Profile

I’m a Doctoral Academy Fellow and PhD student in Civil Engineering (Transportation) at the University of Arkansas. I work at the intersection of multimodal freight systems, data science, and machine learning—building applied tools for resilient, data-driven transportation planning. My recent work with ERDC-USACE involves routing algorithms, AIS-driven analytics, and computer vision for inland waterway operations.

Education

  • Doctor of Philosophy, Civil Engineering (Transportation Engineering), University of Arkansas, Fayetteville — Aug 2024 – May 2028 (in progress), GPA: 3.9/4.0
  • Master of Science, Civil Engineering (Transportation Engineering), University of Arkansas, Fayetteville — Jan 2023 – Aug 2024, GPA: 3.9/4.0
  • Bachelor of Science, Civil Engineering, Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Kumasi, Ghana — Aug 2017 – Nov 2021, First Class Honors

Professional Experience

  • Civil Engineering Intern, Munck Civil Engineering, Doninga, Ghana — May 2019 – Jul 2019

Focus Areas & Tools

  • Multimodal freight systems, long-range planning, transportation safety
  • Machine learning & computer vision (Python, PyTorch), optimization, AIS analytics
  • GIS & spatial analysis (ArcGIS, QGIS), data visualization (Matplotlib, Power BI, Tableau)
